Tips

  • Save your strongest weapons for boss battles instead of using them on weaker enemies.
  • Combine hacking and combat skills together to defeat enemies more efficiently.
  • Upgrade your drone companion to gain support during difficult missions.
  • Complete side quests to earn credits and unlock rare equipment. Learn enemy attack patterns before rushing into combat.
  • Use rooftops and hidden pathways to escape dangerous situations quickly.
  • Keep your health packs stocked before starting major missions.
