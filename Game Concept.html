Storyline

In 2098, the city of Nova Prime is controlled by a corporation called ZenTek Industries. Citizens are monitored by AI surveillance systems and robotic police.

Kai Vex discovers a secret project called “Project Eclipse,” designed to control human minds through cybernetic implants. Players must gather allies, hack security systems, and stop ZenTek before the city loses its freedom forever.

Game Overview

Nova Prime is a futuristic cyberpunk action RPG where players explore a city controlled by AI surveillance and corporate power. As Kai Vex, players must uncover the truth behind Project Eclipse and lead a resistance against ZenTek Industries.

Key Features

  • Massive open-world city
  • Dynamic day and night cycle
  • Advanced hacking mechanics
  • Character customization
  • Multiple story endings
  • Side missions and hidden secrets
  • Player Progression

Players earn experience points by completing missions and defeating enemies. Leveling up unlocks:

  • New combat abilities
  • Hacking upgrades
  • Neural Link: Faster hacking speed
  • Titan Arms: Increased melee damage
  • Optic Scanner: Reveals hidden enemies
  • Reflex Boost: Slows time during combat
  • Avoid AI surveillance cameras
  • Infiltrate secure facilities
  • Solve hacking puzzles
  • Defeat powerful bosses
  • Make difficult moral choices
  • Mission Types
  • Rescue Missions
  • Rewards

Players can earn:

  • Why Nova Prime Needs Saving

ZenTek Industries controls every aspect of life in Nova Prime. Citizens are constantly monitored, and those who oppose the corporation disappear. Project Eclipse threatens to remove all remaining freedom, making Kai's mission humanity's last hope.
