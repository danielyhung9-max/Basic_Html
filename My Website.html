Welcome to Neon Rebellion

"Fight the system. Save the city."

Introduction Neon Rebellion is a futuristic open-world action RPG set in the year 2098. Players explore the massive cyber-city of Nova Prime while battling corrupt corporations, dangerous AI robots, and rival gangs. The player takes the role of Kai Vex, a skilled hacker trying to expose the company controlling the city’s technology.

Features:

  • Open-world exploration
  • Story-driven missions
  • Vehicle customisation
  • Hacking abilities
  • Advanced AI enemies
